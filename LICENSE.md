## What this grant covers

- Original author-generated simulation/task outputs, statistical summaries
  of those simulations, and original data-description/assembly metadata.
- Original author contributions to biological analysis summaries, only to
  the extent the authors hold the rights being licensed. This is NOT a grant
  of unrestricted rights to third-party source content or restricted derivatives.
- The revision's LICENSING_SCOPE.json gives all 296 dataset IDs, unchanged
  content hashes and the simulation/third-party-dependent distinction.
  Crosswalk membership and source aliases document classification, not a
  new legal determination of ownership. Historical byte-identical copies have
  the same scope; other legacy files must be assessed by their actual provenance.

## What this grant does not cover

upstream rights in derived material retain their original terms and attribution.
See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). In particular, do not infer
commercial-use permission for Allen-derived material from our own CC BY grant.
A file described as processed or publicly downloadable is not thereby cleared
of upstream restrictions. Source-specific permission takes precedence over
general provider terms where it applies; uncertain cases require clarification
from the original provider. We do not relicense raw data or waive others' rights.

Code in the separate repository remains GPL-3.0-only. This data licence does

artwork rights. The standard licence text is reproduced from SPDX's licence

modification-notice requirements in a reasonable manner. Please cite both the
dataset/version and the associated article when publishing research that uses
these data; retain relevant upstream citations as well. CITATION.cff supplies
dataset metadata. The associated manuscript is titled *Collective state
conditions the computational value of neuronal complexity*, by Jinhao Zhang,
Jialin Meng and Tianyu Wang. No journal publication, manuscript DOI or dataset
DOI is claimed. Cite the repository and exact commit until archival identifiers
exist. The request to cite the article is scholarly guidance, not an additional
restriction on CC BY rights or a requirement for a particular citation format.
